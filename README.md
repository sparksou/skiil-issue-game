# Skill Issue

*"It's not a bug, you're just bad."*

**Skill Issue** is a minimalist, custom-built 2D precision platformer designed explicitly to psychologically torment players. Built entirely from scratch in Python using Pygame, it rejects standard game-engine hand-holding in favor of ultra-tight, unforgiving physics, deceptive level design, and real-time psychological warfare.

In this game, dying is the main mechanic. The software's primary job is to mock your failures while trapping you in an inescapable cycle of escalating difficulty. 

## How it Works

- **Controls:** Arrow keys or WASD to move, SPACE/UP/W to jump.
- **The Goal:** Reach the green zone at the end of each level.
- **The Catch:** The game learns from you, tracks your deaths, and gets progressively harder the more you play. It includes features like:
  - **Dynamic Insults:** The game actively mocks your failures in 6 different languages.
  - **Sensory Sabotage:** Fake error popups, randomized gaslight sound effects (like Discord pings), and a blinding "Light Mode" designed to induce eye strain.
  - **Mechanical Warfare:** Your controls might randomly invert, and the "Quit" button will literally run away from your mouse cursor.
  - **Shame Ghost:** The game records your worst run and permanently loops it as a gray ghost on the screen as a monument to your struggles.
